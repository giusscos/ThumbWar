## Description [4000]

One, two, three, four, I declare a THUMB WAR!

ThumbestWar turns the classic playground battle into a squishy, puffy 3D showdown. Drag to move, slam to pin, and hold your rival down for a 3-count to win the round.

PIN, WIGGLE, WIN
• Slam down on your rival and hold them for 1, 2, 3!
• Pinned? Wiggle (or mash) to escape before the count ends.
• Hook a slam to flip a pin around at the last second.
• Watch your stamina: tired thumbs tremble and slow down.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Quick Play against the CPU on Easy, Normal or Hard.
• 2 Players on one phone: split screen in portrait, or side by side in landscape.
• Choose timed matches or first to 3 pins.
• Lift-to-slam touch controls, or a big SLAM button.

THE THUMB TOUR
Battle 20 rivals across 4 chapters, from the Backyard Brawl to the Royal Cup. Each rival has their own style, rules and signature hat. Earn up to 3 stars per challenge and collect coins along the way.

DRESS TO IMPRESS
Spend coins in the Locker on 30+ hats and face accessories: cowboy hats, viking helmets, monocles, mustaches, a wizard hat and more. Spooky Halloween items are in the Locker now.

TROPHIES & LEADERBOARDS
Unlock 20 achievements and climb the Game Center leaderboards for Thumb Tour stars, lifetime pins and your best win streak.

No ads. No account. Just thumbs.
